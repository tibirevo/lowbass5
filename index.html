ELEVATE YOUR SOUND

Located in Deva, Romania, Lowbass5 is a full-size recording studio dedicated to capturing pristine audio and fueling creative vision. Founded and operated by Adrian "Bila," the studio provides a professional, fully equipped environment for artists, bands, and producers to record, mix, and elevate their sound to the highest standard.

ABOUT

Established in 2010 in Deva, Romania, Lowbass5 Studio is a full-size, professional recording, mixing, and mastering facility designed to bring out the absolute best in every artist. What started as a dedicated creative base has evolved over the past decade and a half into one of the most respected independent production hubs in the region, particularly renowned within the rock, metal, and alternative music scenes.

Meet the Engineer: Adrian "Bila" Urițescu

At the heart of Lowbass5 is founder, producer, and sound engineer Adrian "Bila" Urițescu. With nearly two decades of hands-on experience both behind the studio console and on stage, Bila understands music from the inside out. As a longtime guitarist and bassist for the hardcore/metal band TARG3T, as well as the FOH and monitor engineer for internationally acclaimed folkcore giants Dirty Shirt, he brings a battle-tested ear and a musician’s intuition to every project.

The Studio & Philosophy

Whether tracking booming live drums and crushing guitar tones, or dialing in crisp vocals, hard-hitting beats, and radio-ready pop and hip-hop mixes, Lowbass5 Studio combines top-tier acoustic treatment with high-end analog and digital gear. Over the years, the studio has shaped the sound of an extensive roster of bands and solo artists—including Dirty Shirt, TARG3T, Revolter, Doomsday Astronaut, Harmasar, Gri, and FronT, among many others.

At Lowbass5, the goal is simple: no generic presets, no rushed takes—just an inspiring, comfortable environment where artists of any genre can push their boundaries and achieve a massive, industry-standard sound.
